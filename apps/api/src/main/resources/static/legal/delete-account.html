How to request deletion

To delete your YoCabs account and the personal data associated with it, send an email to yocabssupport@gmail.com from the mobile number or email address linked to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account". Include your registered mobile number so we can find your account.

We will confirm your identity, process the request, and let you know by email once it is complete. We aim to complete every request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens to it
Name, mobile number, profile detailsDeleted
Saved places, app preferencesDeleted
Device push notification tokenDeleted
Live trip location historyAlready not retained once a trip ends
Completed booking and payment records Kept for the period required by Indian tax, accounting, and consumer-dispute law, then deleted. These records are kept even after account deletion because we are legally required to retain them, not because we need your continued consent.

Requesting only some of your data be deleted

If you want specific data removed (for example, a saved address) without deleting your whole account, email the same address and describe what you'd like removed - we will action what we are able to without deleting records we are legally required to keep.
