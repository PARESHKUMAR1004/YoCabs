1. What this Policy covers

This Policy explains what personal data YoCabs collects through the mobile app, why, and what rights you have over it. "We", "us", and "YoCabs" refer to the operator identified in our Terms of Service.

2. Data we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Name and mobile numberAccount creation and sign-in (OTP verification)
Pickup, destination, and stop locationsTo search for cabs and calculate a fare
Live location during an active tripSo you can follow your assigned driver, and so the driver can be tracked for safety
Payment transaction detailsTo process your booking token and balance payment (card/UPI/bank details themselves are handled by our payment gateway, never stored by us)
Push notification tokenTo send you booking, trip, and payment updates
Support messagesTo respond to a support ticket you raise
Vehicle/document photos (travel partners only)To verify a partner's vehicle before it is listed

3. Who we share it with

We share only what each of the following needs to do its job:

  • Payment gateway - to process your booking payments and refunds.
  • Mapping and routing providers - to calculate distance, duration, and directions for a trip.
  • SMS provider - to deliver your one-time login code.
  • Push notification provider - to deliver in-app and device notifications.
  • The travel partner assigned to your trip - your pickup/destination and contact number, so the trip can be carried out.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, for any purpose.

4. How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records for as long as needed to resolve disputes, meet tax and accounting obligations, and comply with law - typically several years after a trip. Live trip location is not retained once a trip ends and is no longer needed for the safety purpose it was collected for.

5. Your rights

Under applicable Indian data protection law, you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data, where we are not required to keep it for legal or accounting reasons
  • Withdraw consent to non-essential processing (this may limit what the app can do for you)

To exercise any of these, write to [support/privacy email]. We will respond within the time required by law.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls, and signed, time-limited links for sensitive documents like your trip bill) to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you if a breach affects your data as required by law.

7. Children's privacy

YoCabs is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. We will highlight material changes in the app.
